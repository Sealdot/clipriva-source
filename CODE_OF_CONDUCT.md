# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the ClipRiva community a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity or expression, experience level, education, socio-economic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We will act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and
healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Demonstrate empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Be respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Give and gracefully accept constructive feedback.
- Accept responsibility and apologize to people affected by mistakes.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the project's users.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized language or imagery; trolling, insulting or derogatory
comments; public or private harassment; publishing others' private information without permission;
and other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project Issues, Pull Requests, Discussions, official communities,
and online or in-person events where someone represents ClipRiva.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the security contact described in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not include sensitive clipboard content in a report.

Project maintainers will review reports fairly and may remove comments, reject contributions,
temporarily restrict participation, or take other appropriate action. Retaliation against people
who report or help investigate a concern is not acceptable.
